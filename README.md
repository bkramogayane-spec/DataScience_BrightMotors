# 🚗 Bright Motors – Car Sales Analysis (Databricks Case Study)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a data analytics case study completed for **Bright Motors**, aimed at supporting a newly appointed **Head of Sales** with actionable insights derived from historical car sales data.

The analysis focuses on understanding:
- Revenue drivers by car make and model
- The relationship between price, mileage, and year of manufacture
- Sales performance by seller and region
- Profitability and margin optimisation opportunities

The solution was implemented using **Databricks (SQL )**, following modern data engineering and analytics best practices.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this case study are to:

- Identify **top-performing car makes and models**
- Analyse how **vehicle age and mileage impact pricing**
- Determine **high-performing sellers and regions**
- Classify vehicles by **profit margin tiers**
- Provide **data-driven recommendations** to improve dealership profitability and efficiency
